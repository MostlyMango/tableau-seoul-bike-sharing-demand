\# 🚲 Seoul Bike Sharing Demand Analysis



\## Overview



This project analyzes one year of bike-sharing demand in Seoul, South Korea using Tableau Desktop.



The objective was to identify how weather conditions and temporal factors influence hourly bike rental demand and translate those findings into actionable business recommendations.



\---



\## Business Questions



\- Which weather variables have the strongest relationship with hourly bike rental demand?



\- How does bike demand vary across hours, seasons, and holidays?



\---



\## Tools Used



\- Tableau Desktop

\- Tableau Prep

\- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

\## Key Findings



\- Temperature has a strong positive relationship with bike rentals.

\- Rainfall decreases bike demand.

\- Snowfall has the strongest negative impact.

\- Demand peaks around 8 AM and 6 PM.

\- Summer records the highest bike usage.
